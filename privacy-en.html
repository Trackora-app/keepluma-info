01

Publisher and contact

Publisher: TRACKORA. Privacy, data access and account deletion requests: trackora.app@gmail.com. Last updated: 3 October 2026.

02

Data used

We process your email and authentication information; tracker titles, categories, dates, notes, reminders and additional details; history, usage and cycle records; and uploaded document files. Health or private information you choose to enter is included in your records. Your display name, language, theme and notification preferences are kept on this device. Session information is stored on the device to keep you signed in.

03

Purpose and infrastructure

Data enables sign-in, record storage, document access, reminders and PDF/JSON export. Supabase provides authentication, database and document storage. The current project's server region is Sydney, Australia. Network transfers use HTTPS. Password verification is performed through Supabase Auth.

04

Sharing and payments

Data is processed in Supabase infrastructure to provide the service. TRACKORA does not sell personal data. This test version has no advertising SDK or active in-app payments. Exports include records marked as sensitive; you decide whether to share your exported files.

05

Retention and deletion

Active account records are retained while you use the account; you can delete individual records in the app. Account deletion removes the active account and associated trackers, history, reminder records, profile and uploaded document files. Infrastructure backups and technical logs are subject to the service provider's retention terms. Previously downloaded/exported files and copies shared with others are not automatically removed.

06

Your choices

Use Profile > Export data to save your records as PDF or JSON. Attached PDF/photo files are not included; importing is not supported yet. Profile > Delete account requires your current password and final confirmation. Notifications can be disabled in profile settings. Contact support for data access, correction or deletion requests; identity verification may be required.
