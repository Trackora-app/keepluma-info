Account and Data Deletion

Last updated · 3 October 2026
Privacy policyAccount deletion
01

Delete from the app

Sign in to TRACKORA. Open Profile > Delete account. Export your records first if needed. Enter your current password, type DELETE and choose Delete permanently in the final confirmation.

02

If you cannot access the app

From your registered email address, email trackora.app@gmail.com with the subject 'TRACKORA account deletion request' and request deletion of your account and associated data. Never include your password or session tokens. Account ownership is verified to prevent unauthorized deletion. This is a support request, not an automatic or immediate deletion.

03

Data deleted and retained

The active account, profile, trackers, history, reminder records and uploaded documents are deleted. This cannot be undone. Previously exported/downloaded files remain on your device and must be removed separately. Infrastructure backups and technical logs are subject to the service provider's retention terms.
